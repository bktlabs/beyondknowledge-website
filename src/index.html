Beyond Knowledge Technologies is an independent Swiss company working at the foundations of advanced software. We bring together fundamental research, innovative architecture, and practical engineering to address challenges that demand technical depth.

Our experience includes academic research in zero-knowledge cryptography, recursive proofs, decentralized protocols, and zero-knowledge virtual machines (zkVMs).

Based in Ticino and working internationally, we collaborate with researchers and engineering teams to move from first principles to robust implementations.
